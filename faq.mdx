## What does Omenra actually build?

Intelligence that anticipates: systems that produce a claim about the future with a probability, a horizon and a consequence — and then act on it inside a contract. In practice that means five kinds of artefact: [agents](/births/agents), [models](/births/models), [tools](/births/tools), [bots](/births/bots) and [robots](/births/robots).

## How do you pronounce it?

**oh-MEN-rah.** Stress on the middle syllable. Omen + era.

## Is there a product I can sign up for?

Not yet. Omenra is in design with a first implementation in progress. There is no public SDK, no hosted product and no pricing. See [Where we are](/company/about#where-we-are).

## So why publish documentation?

Because documentation written before the product is the cheapest way to discover which definitions are wrong. Every page here is one we expect to revise, and the people who tell us what is broken are the people who will build on it.

## Is Omenra trying to build AGI?

We are trying to build systems that see what comes next and act well inside a boundary. Whether that path leads to general intelligence is a question we do not get to answer by asserting it. Language models got dramatically better at reasoning while getting no better at knowing when they are wrong — that gap is the actual work.

## Unpredictable agents sound dangerous. Which is it?

Both, which is why the contract exists. Inside its scope an agent should find strategies nobody specified — that is where the value is. Outside its scope it has no permissions at all, because credentials are issued per birth and per action class. See [Safety](/control/safety).

## Do you build robots?

Eventually, and last. Embodiment is where mistakes cannot be rolled back, so it ships after simulation, supervision and a safety envelope enforced below the planning layer. See [Robots](/births/robots).

## Is it open source?

Not currently. The vocabulary and standards in this documentation are published openly because they are more useful shared than held. Licensing for the platform itself is undecided.

## Can I use it on my own infrastructure?

Self-hosted deployment is planned for partners whose data cannot leave their network. It is on the [roadmap](/company/roadmap) rather than available today.

## What kinds of problem are a good fit?

Problems where the tool choice is not obvious in advance, the environment changes, and a human currently makes the judgement by hand. Triage, escalation, demand and supply, maintenance, clinical flow, fraud interception. Not: deterministic pipelines, or anything requiring a legal right of appeal on an automated decision.

## What is explicitly out of scope?

Autonomous lethal action; predictions about identifiable people used to deny them credit, employment, insurance or liberty; election influence; and any system that can widen its own permissions. The full list is on [Safety](/control/safety#what-we-will-not-build).

## How do I work with you?

Email **hello@omenra.si** with one paragraph: the prediction, the horizon, and what happens when you are wrong. See [Design partners](/company/about#design-partners).

## Is any of this live in production?

No. If you need a system in production this quarter, we are not the right partner yet — and we would rather say so now than six weeks into a pilot.
