## The name

**Omenra** = *omen* + *era* — the age in which signs are read.

An omen is a sign of what is coming. An era is a long stretch of time. Put together, the name says the two things the company is about: **foresight**, and **duration** — systems built to last longer than a model release cycle.

It is chosen for practical reasons too:

| Property | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **oh-MEN-rah** — three syllables, no ambiguity | People repeat it correctly after hearing it once |
| No hard consonants, no silent letters | Pronounceable in English, Hindi, Slovenian and most of Europe |
| Sounds like a company name, not a product name | Products inherit a name; the company keeps it |
| `.si` and `.com` available as a pair | A domain that does not cost a fundraise |

The domain is `omenra.si`; these docs live at `docs.omenra.si`.

## The premise

Omenra exists to build intelligence that anticipates. Not chat, not search, not another dashboard over the past — systems that produce a claim about the future with a probability, a horizon and a consequence, and then act on it inside a contract.

That work requires five kinds of artefact, not one: [agents](/births/agents), [models](/births/models), [tools](/births/tools), [bots](/births/bots) and [robots](/births/robots). We call creating one a **birth**, because the thing keeps changing after it ships.

## Principles

**Write the contract before the prompt.** Boundaries are cheap to draw and expensive to retrofit.

**Calibration before cleverness.** A system that knows how unsure it is can be trusted with more than one that is merely impressive.

**Containment is code.** If a safety property depends on the model choosing well, it is not a safety property.

**Publish the standard, not the press release.** This documentation says what we will and will not build, including the parts that are uncomfortable.

**Small team, sharp vocabulary.** Six words in the [glossary](/glossary) carry the whole platform. If two of them collapse into one in a conversation, something is being built wrong.

## Where we are

<Note>
  Omenra is at the beginning: a platform design, a vocabulary, and a first implementation in progress. No public SDK, no hosted product, no shipped robots. This documentation is published early so that the people who will build on it can argue with it.
</Note>

That candour is a strategy, not a disclaimer. Documentation written before the product is the cheapest way to find out which definitions are wrong — and every page here is one we expect to revise.

## Design partners

The first cohort is small and hands-on. A design partner gets:

- a first birth built with us, against a real prediction problem in your business,
- influence over the vocabulary and the contract model before they freeze,
- direct access to the people writing the platform,
- the evaluation set from your problem as an artefact you keep.

What we ask in return: a real problem with a stated horizon, a person who owns the outcome, and permission to be told plainly when the design is wrong.
